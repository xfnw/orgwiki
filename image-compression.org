* remarks
avif was the best for every image, av1 ftw!

except for png8 and gif, which look like ass, all the formats look
fine to my untrained fox eyeballs.

i keep hearing people say jxl is better at compression than webp, but
for every image i have ever lossfully converted to both jxl and webp,
the webp has been the smaller one. one jxl was even larger than the
original jpg it came from! is everyone just repeating bullshit they've
heard without checking for themselves? or is imagemagick's jxl encoder
just that uniquely bad?
